## Problem Title
SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers earnings and increase consumer prices.

## Problem Creater's Organization
Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

## Theme
Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

## Problem Description
The agricultural supply chain in India involves several intermediaries between farmers and consumers. Farmers often receive lower prices for their produce, while consumers pay higher prices due to commissions, transportation costs and unnecessary distribution stages.

Small-scale farmers also face difficulties in identifying buyers, understanding market prices, arranging transportation and accessing larger markets.

Main challenges:
1. Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers' profit margins.
2. Consumers pay higher prices for agricultural products.
3. Farmers lack access to real-time market information.
4. Transportation and storage facilities are limited.
5. Lack of transparent pricing creates difficulties for farmers and buyers.

Benefits:

• Better prices for farmers.
• Lower prices for consumers.
• Reduced supply chain inefficiencies.

## Proposed Solution
FarmBridge is a smart digital marketplace designed to eliminate unnecessary intermediaries in the agricultural supply chain by connecting farmers directly with consumers and bulk buyers.

Farmers can register on the platform, upload their available products, specify quantities and set prices based on current market information. Buyers can browse products, compare prices, place orders and make secure digital payments.

The platform uses AI-based demand forecasting to predict product demand and suggest suitable selling prices. It also provides shared logistics facilities by grouping orders from nearby farmers and arranging combined transportation to reduce delivery costs.

Key features:
1. Direct Marketplace: Connects farmers directly with consumers, retailers, hotels and bulk purchasers.
2. Smart Price Recommendation: Suggests fair selling prices using market trends and historical price information.
3. AI Demand Forecasting: Predicts demand for agricultural products to help farmers plan their sales.
4. Shared Logistics: Groups nearby orders and coordinates transportation to reduce delivery expenses.
5. Digital Quality Verification: Allows buyers to view product details, photographs, harvest dates and quality grades.
6. Secure Payments: Supports digital transactions and provides payment status tracking.
7. Multilingual Interface: Supports regional languages to make the platform accessible to farmers.
How it addresses the problem:
- Reduces dependency on unnecessary intermediaries.
- Improves farmers' selling opportunities and earnings.
- Makes agricultural products more affordable for consumers.
- Reduces transportation expenses through shared delivery.
- Provides transparent pricing and transaction information.
Innovation and uniqueness:

The major innovation of FarmBridge is its combination of AI-based demand prediction, community-based logistics and direct digital trading in one platform.

Unlike a basic agricultural shopping application, FarmBridge helps farmers not only find buyers but also decide when to sell, understand market prices and arrange affordable transportation.

Methodology and implementation
Step 1 – Farmer Registration
Farmers create accounts and provide their basic details, location and agricultural information.
Step 2 – Product Listing
Farmers upload product names, available quantities, photographs, harvest dates and expected prices.
Step 3 – AI Analysis
The system analyses historical market prices, product availability and demand patterns to recommend suitable prices and predict demand.
Step 4 – Buyer Connection
Consumers and bulk buyers search for products, compare prices and place orders directly with farmers.
Step 5 – Order Management
The system confirms orders and updates the availability of agricultural products.
Step 6 – Shared Logistics
The platform identifies nearby orders and groups deliveries to reduce transportation costs.
Step 7 – Payment and Delivery
Buyers complete digital payments through an integrated payment gateway. Farmers and buyers can track order and payment status.

## Feasibility and Viability
Feasibility of the idea:
FarmBridge is technically feasible because it uses existing technologies such as Python, Django, MySQL and machine learning libraries.

The platform can initially be developed as a web application and later expanded into a mobile application.

Potential challenges and risks:
1. Limited digital knowledge among some farmers.
2. Difficulty in maintaining accurate product quality information.
3. Transportation coordination between different locations.
4. Fluctuations in agricultural prices and demand.
5. Internet connectivity issues in rural areas.
Strategies to overcome challenges:
1. Provide a simple interface with regional language support.
2. Introduce product verification and buyer feedback.
3. Partner with local transport providers and farmer groups.
4. Regularly update market price information.
5. Develop a lightweight application that works with limited internet connectivity.

## Impact and Benefits
Potential impact on the target audience:
- Farmers can reach more buyers and improve their selling opportunities.
- Consumers can access fresh agricultural products at competitive prices.
- Retailers and bulk buyers can source products directly from farmers.
